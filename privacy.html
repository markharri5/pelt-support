Pelt! Snowball Showdown – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Pelt! Snowball Showdown does not collect, store or share any personal information.

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. TWO PLAYER mode talks only to a friend's phone in the same room, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, and nothing is sent anywhere else.

Your high scores are saved on your device. If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best scores are also sent to Game Center's world, weekly and country leaderboards, where they appear under your Game Center nickname. The country is taken from your device's region setting, not your location. Game Center is provided by Apple, not by us, and we never receive your name, Apple Account or any other personal details. Apple's own privacy policy covers Game Center, and parents can turn Game Center off in Screen Time.

How long things are kept, and how to remove them. Scores, coins and grotto items are stored only on your device, for as long as the app is installed; deleting the app removes them all. Scores sent to Game Center are kept by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; turning off Game Center (in Settings, or in Screen Time for a child) stops any more being sent. Purchases are made through Apple, and we never see payment details.

If this ever changes (for example, if ads are added), this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Questions? See the support page.
